## Full description (max 4000 characters)

ANJANAM is your all-in-one shopping app for physical products and digital content — built for India with secure Razorpay payments.

**Shop physical products**
Browse categories, compare prices, add to cart, and checkout in minutes. Track orders, manage returns, and get support when you need it.

**Digital content**
- Read articles in a secure in-app reader
- Watch curated videos and YouTube content
- Explore books and music in the storefront

**Why ANJANAM**
- Fast, modern shopping experience
- Secure payments via Razorpay
- Order history and delivery tracking
- Share products, videos, and articles with friends
- Wishlist and personalized recommendations

**Account & support**
Sign in with phone or Google. Manage your profile, addresses, and order history. Need help? Contact support@anjanam.store or visit https://anjanam.store/support

**Also on the web**
Shop at https://anjanam.store — same account works on web and Android.

Download ANJANAM and start shopping today.
