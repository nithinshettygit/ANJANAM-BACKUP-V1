Delete Account

You can request deletion of your ANJANAM account and personal data at any time.

To request account deletion

  1. Send an email to support@anjanam.store with subject: Request account deletion.
  2. Include your registered phone number or email used in the app.

What we delete

  • Account profile details and contact data tied to your account.
  • Personal identifiers associated with your account activity.
  • Order data may be deleted where legally allowed, or otherwise anonymized.

Data retention

Some records may be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or accounting obligations for up to 180 days, after which they are deleted or irreversibly anonymized as required by law.

Delete some data without deleting account

No. We currently process deletion requests as full account deletion requests.
